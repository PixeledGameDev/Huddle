Default controls   P1: A/D move, W up, S down, Space or W jump, F (or J) attack, G (or K) special. P2: arrows move, comma or up arrow jump, period attack, slash special. Hold up or down with attack for other moves (Settings: UP TO JUMP turns W/up-arrow jumping off). Esc pauses; remap in Settings. Click the game once if keys do nothing.
